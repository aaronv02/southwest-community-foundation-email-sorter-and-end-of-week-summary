TALKING TO CLAUDE ABOUT YOUR EMAIL
==================================

Once connected, you can open Claude and ask about your Outlook in plain
English. You don't need to learn any commands - just say what you want.


FIRST, AFTER CONNECTING
-----------------------

Quit Claude completely and reopen it. Closing the window is not enough:
right-click the Claude icon in the system tray (near the clock, bottom-right)
and choose Quit, then open it again.

You'll know it worked if you ask "what emails am I forgetting?" and it answers
with real messages instead of saying it can't see your email.


THINGS TO ASK
-------------

Catching up

    "what emails am I forgetting?"
    "who is waiting on me?"
    "anything I've left hanging more than a week?"
    "how was my week?"
    "catch me up, I was out Tuesday and Wednesday"

Finding things

    "anything from Tessa this month?"
    "what did I tell the board about the audit?"
    "show me unread mail about grants"
    "did I ever reply to that bequest question?"

Planning

    "what's coming up next week?"
    "any meetings I haven't responded to?"
    "what do I need to prepare for?"

Sorting

    "sort my emails"
    "sort the next 50"

Drafting - this is where it gets genuinely useful

    "what am I forgetting? draft replies to the top three"
    "read the Mancos Valley one and help me answer it"
    "write a polite holding reply to the reporter saying I'll
     get back to her Monday"

It can read a full message and help you write the answer. It cannot send
anything - you copy the draft into Outlook yourself.


ABOUT SORTING
-------------

When you say "sort my emails", Claude reads recent unlabelled mail and
proposes a colored label for each one. It shows you the list first.

Nothing changes until you say yes.

If it gets one wrong, just tell it: "no, the Ballantine one is a scholarship
thing" and it will correct that before applying. You can also change any label
in Outlook afterwards the normal way.

It works in batches of about 50 so it doesn't use up your Claude allowance in
one go. Say "sort the next 50" to continue.

The labels are:

    Donors & Gifts        donors, gifts, fund questions, planned giving
    Grants                LOIs, applications, grant reports, LAUNCH Fund
    Scholarships          students, committees, disbursements, schools
    Board & Governance    agendas, minutes, policies, board business
    Finance               statements, payouts, invoices, bookkeeping, 990
    Events                Wine Experience, Hoedown, 19th Hole, sponsors
    Nonprofit Partners    fiscal sponsorship, workshops, capacity building
    Press & Community     reporters, interviews, awards, speaking
    Sector News           Council on Foundations, Candid, newsletters
    Vendors & Admin       software, IT, office, subscriptions
    Needs Review          it wasn't sure - worth a look

If those categories aren't right for how you actually work, say so. They can
be renamed or changed.


WHAT IT CANNOT DO
-----------------

It cannot send email, delete anything, or move messages between folders. The
only change it can make is adding a colored label, and only after you agree.

It only sees what's in Outlook. Phone calls, site visits, and anything in the
grants database are invisible to it. It knows how you replied to a meeting
invite, not whether you actually went.

It only works while Claude is open on this computer. Your Friday summary email
is separate and arrives whether or not Claude is running.


IF IT STOPS WORKING
-------------------

Ask Claude "what tools do you have?" - it should list things like
whats_waiting and sort. If it doesn't:

  1. Quit Claude completely (system tray, right-click, Quit) and reopen it.
  2. If that doesn't fix it, double-click "Connect To Claude.cmd" again.
  3. If it still fails, there's a log at:
       %APPDATA%\SWCFDigest\digest.log
     Send the last few lines to whoever set this up.


ABOUT YOUR PRIVACY
------------------

When you ask Claude about your email, the messages it reads become part of
that conversation - the same as if you had pasted them in yourself. That
includes donor names and amounts when those appear in the mail it reads.

If you'd rather keep a particular thread out of it, don't ask about it.
